Far apart, something in common

How to read a label

Every cuisine gets a label laid out like the Nutrition Facts on a box. Instead of nutrients it lists dishes, ingredients, flavors, techniques and the peoples, empires and trade that shaped it.

Where a food label prints % Daily Value, this one prints Shared: how many of the 12 cuisines in the chart list the same item. Scope says whether the entry is one country's cooking or a family of them. Sources says what the facts rest on; none are cited yet, and the label says so.
