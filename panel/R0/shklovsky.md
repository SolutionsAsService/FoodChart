# Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky) - R0

- **Anchor used:** Viktor Shklovsky, "Art as Technique" (1917): automatization "devours works, clothes, furniture, one's wife, and the fear of war"; art exists to restore the sensation of things by making forms "unfamiliar" and prolonging the difficulty of perception.
- **Observed:** The inherited data is pure automatization: food reduced to a stock-phrase list. "Famous dishes", "Popular dishes", "Cooking techniques" are labels standing in for perception, not perceptions. "Spicy flavors", "Rich sauces", "Diverse flavors" are the clichés a reader already holds before reading. "Regional diversity" is listed as an influence for Chinese and French, and "Minimalist Presentation" as a Japanese technique: the categories are worn smooth and interchangeable. Grouping like "Spices (Turmeric, Cumin, Coriander, Cardamom)" and "Herbs (Thyme, Rosemary)" folds specific tastes into a generic algebraic noun. "Middle Eastern" is one entry for a vast region. The name "FoodChart" promises a chart and nothing is charted; the repo has no page and no README that CONTRIBUTING.md points to. The device (a flat list) never makes the reader see anything.
- **Verdict:** OBJECT
- **Requested change:** Do not just tidy the lists into prettier cliches. (1) Make the chart the defamiliarizing device: show cuisines by shared ingredient, technique and influence so unexpected kinship appears (Thai and Italian both lean on fresh herbs; Chinese and Japanese on steaming), and let the reader work to read it. (2) Replace placeholders with one concrete, specific sensory detail each (a named dish, an actual ingredient, a real texture) rather than "Popular dishes". (3) Give every cuisine page a strange, particular angle, not a summary of adjectives. (4) Split the junk categories so influences, techniques and flavors stay distinct, and keep ingredients atomic so they can be compared. (5) Mark Middle Eastern as a region with a visible note. (6) Don't hide the defamiliarization behind friction that blocks use: the navigation stays plain, the strangeness lives in the chart.
- **Statement of meaning:** The artifact currently says that food is a list of things everyone already knows, and nobody has looked at any of it.
