# R1 Critic: Cross-Cultural (Escobar)

- Anchor used: the pluriverse ("a world where many worlds fit"), against a one-world design that sorts every kitchen into one scheme, plus autonomous design (communities define their own categories).

- Observed:
  - Strong: the site names its own limits. about.html: "Twelve entries cannot hold the world's cooking... 'Middle Eastern' here stands for a family". Every label has "Sources: None cited yet"; "Not on the chart yet" lists 12 absent kitchens; kinds are "judgments... open to correction"; the Shared* footnote refuses to count the world; cooks are invited to correct.
  - Weak: the one-world frame. Every cuisine is a row in one template (dishes, ingredients, flavors, techniques) with one Western taxonomy of influence (roots, trade, empire, colonial rule). Europe has 5 of 12 entries; West Asia and North Africa has one, and all of Africa has none except "African" as an influence on Mexican.
  - influences.html lists "Chinese" under Trade and exchange, Migration, and Empire and conquest with the same four cuisines (4 of 12 each time), so Japanese, Korean, Thai and Vietnamese kitchens appear only as receivers. "Chinese is the most shared" reads as a ranking of whose world counts.
  - The sole Forced migration entry is "African", a continent, not the people who cooked; Mexico's "Indigenous (Aztec, Maya)" sits under Roots, in the past tense.
  - about.html: "Garlic is used in all twelve kitchens" is an unsourced claim stated as fact.

- Verdict: OBJECT

- Requested change (all doable now, no new research):
  1. On influences.html, show each influence once, with its kinds listed together (Chinese: trade, migration, empire), not repeated in three groups that triple its count.
  2. Rename "African" to name the agents, e.g. "Enslaved Africans' cooking", and say in the sentence under the h1 who judged the kinds (one author, outside these kitchens).
  3. Add to about.html: the categories, the template and the "influence" frame are this project's, not these cuisines' own.
  4. Soften the garlic sentence to "likely used in all twelve" or remove it.

- Statement of meaning: This is a modest, honest sketch by one outsider that admits its categories are borrowed, yet still lets one template and one family tree of influence stand in for twelve worlds.
