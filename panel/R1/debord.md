**Report: Critic: Provocateur (Debord), R1**

- **Anchor used:** detournement. The site takes the commodity's own form, the Nutrition Facts label, and turns it against the commodity's claim to speak for the thing it packages.

- **Observed:**
  - The detournement works in several places. "Twelve cuisines, read like the back of a box" announces the hijack openly.
  - The "Shared*" column sits where "% Daily Value" would be, and its footnote says "a count from this chart's data, not a measure of the world's cooking".
  - "Sources: None cited yet" and "Curated and incomplete" expose the spectacle's pretense of authority.
  - The "Unexpected neighbors" block (Chinese and Thai, Greek and Middle Eastern) is a derive: it joins items the market's national shelving keeps apart.
  - The chart comes first on the home page, and an unfilled cell is a faint dot.
  - On the Mexican label, "Shaped by" lists "Indigenous (Aztec, Maya) Roots", "Spanish Colonial rule" and "African Forced migration and enslavement" as three identical lines.
  - Vietnamese: "French Colonial rule" has the same weight as "Chinese Empire and conquest".
  - The label's rhythm of line item, tag and count erases the difference between an ingredient and a conquest. This is the commodity's equivalence, and it is the spectacle's flattening at work.
  - Nothing on the label says who cooked, only what was listed. Labor is invisible.

- **Verdict:** OBJECT. Only the "Shaped by" block.

- **Requested change:** On every label and on the influences page, set the violent kinds (Empire and conquest, Colonial rule, Forced migration and enslavement) apart from Roots, Trade and exchange, and Migration. Use a heavy rule and a sub-label such as "Imposed" above them, so the label's smooth form breaks where the history does. The influences page is already grouped by kind. The labels themselves should carry that grouping too.

- **Statement of meaning:** The site says that every cuisine can be priced and shelved like a product, then shows the label's own blanks (no sources, a partial sample) to expose that claim, and it should stop letting conquest read as just another ingredient.
